01 · The Foundation

The 3 types of AI in SAP

SAP combines three complementary forms of artificial intelligence across the SuccessFactors suite — each solving a different class of HR problem.

📈

Predictive AI

Anticipate

Uses data patterns to forecast outcomes before they happen — so HR can act early rather than react late.

Example: Predicting employee flight risk and modelling future workforce demand.
💬

Generative AI — Joule

Assist

SAP's AI copilot answers questions, drafts content, and simplifies HR tasks through natural language.

Example: Ask Joule to explain a policy, draft a job description, or summarise a team in plain language.
⚙️

Agentic AI

Automate

Autonomous AI agents that execute multi-step tasks end to end, without human intervention at each step.

Example: Running full onboarding journeys and processing payroll automatically.
