The three types of AI in SAP

SAP combines three complementary forms of artificial intelligence across SuccessFactors. Each one addresses a different class of HR problem.

01
Anticipate

Predictive AI

Reads patterns in your data to forecast outcomes before they happen, so HR can act early instead of reacting late.

In practice: flagging employee flight risk and modelling future workforce demand.
02
Assist

Generative AI, Joule

SAP's AI copilot answers questions, drafts content, and simplifies everyday HR tasks through plain, natural language.

In practice: explaining a policy, drafting a job description, or summarising a team on request.
03
Automate

Agentic AI

Autonomous AI agents execute multi-step tasks from start to finish, without a human stepping in at every stage.

In practice: running full onboarding journeys and processing payroll end to end.
