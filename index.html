Section 01 — The foundation

The three types of AI in SAP

SAP combines three complementary forms of artificial intelligence across SuccessFactors — each addressing a distinct class of HR problem.

01
Anticipate

Predictive AI

Reads patterns in existing data to forecast outcomes before they occur — so HR can act early rather than react late.

ExamplePredicting employee flight risk and modelling future workforce demand.
02
Assist

Generative AI — Joule

SAP's AI copilot answers questions, drafts content, and simplifies HR tasks through natural language.

ExampleAsk Joule to explain a policy, draft a job description, or summarise a team in plain language.
03
Automate

Agentic AI

Autonomous AI agents that execute multi-step tasks end to end, without human intervention at each step.

ExampleRunning full onboarding journeys and processing payroll automatically.
